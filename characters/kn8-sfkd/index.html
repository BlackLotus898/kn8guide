// Status Effects

Ailments & Special Status

Transcendence Gauge

A resource unique to this unit, max 15 stacks. Builds from wave start (+5) and from allies' Normal Attack/Combat Skill use (+1 Attacker, +2 Support, +1 Defender). At max, enables Phase Transition state.

Phase Transition

Entered once the Transcendence Gauge reaches its max of 15. All of this unit's skills change into Transcendence Skills, and Lightning DMG dealt +30%. Advances this unit's next Action Order by 100% on entry, then runs on a fixed-SPD-70 countdown before reverting.
