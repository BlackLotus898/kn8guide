Ailments & Special Status

Momentum

Gained after an ally activates Extra Attack or after this unit activates Ultimate. At max stacks, this unit's Ultimate changes into Enhanced Ultimate. Consumed after activating Enhanced Ultimate.

Devotion

Increases this unit's Extra Attack DMG Multiplier. If at 2 or more stacks, after activating Combat Skill or Ultimate, activates Extra Attack and consumes 2 stacks.
