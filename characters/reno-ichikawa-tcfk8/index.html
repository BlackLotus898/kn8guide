Passive Skill

Uncontrollable Power

  • At the start of battle, Froststorm Turn appears.
  • Froststorm Turn: 1. Fixed SPD of 60. 2. After turn arrives, activates Overflow Attack. If unable to take action, Overflow Attack does not activate.
  • Overflow Attack: 1. Deals Slash and Ice DMG equal to 88% of this unit's ATK to a single Kaiju. 2. Deals Slash and Ice DMG equal to 22% of this unit's ATK to adjacent Kaiju.
  • Each time an ally attacks a Kaiju with Normal Attack, Combat Skill, or Ultimate, applies 1 stack of Chill to this unit. Max 40 stacks.
  • After activating Combat Skill, if Chill is at its max stacks: 1. Applies Overflow Attack DMG Multiplier +22% (1 turn) to this unit. 2. Advances Froststorm Turn's next Action Order by 100%. 3. Consumes 40 stacks of Chill.
  • Transcendence Gauge: Max 15. After an ally attacks, this unit's Transcendence Gauge +1.
Additional Passive Skills

// Status Effects

Ailments & Special Status

Absolute Frost

Granted by Transcendence Arts. All skills change into Transcended Skills, increasing Ice DMG dealt and CRIT Rate.

Chill

Stacks (max 40) gained by this unit's skills and allies' attacks. At max stacks after Combat Skill, boosts Overflow Attack DMG and advances Froststorm Turn, consuming the stacks.

Froststorm Turn

Appears at the start of battle with a fixed SPD of 60 and performs an Overflow Attack on its turn.
