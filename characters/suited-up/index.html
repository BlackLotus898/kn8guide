// Status Effects

Ailments & Special Status

Conduction

Increases the target's ATK. The amount of ATK increase is based on this unit's ATK (start of battle). After activating Combat Skill or Ultimate, deals Lightning Additional DMG.

Supercritical

All skills change into Transcendence Skills. Increases all allies' DMG dealt. Increases All-Attribute DMG dealt for allies in Transcendence state.
